## PROTOCOL

1. Fragment coral into small chunks.

2. Transfer fragment to a tube, taking care to avoid powder. Add 1,000 uL 15% EDTA (0.5M, pH 8) to fully submerge coral fragment.

Decalcification

3. Incubate overnight in 1,000 uL of 15% EDTA (0.5M, pH 5.2) at 4˚C. 

> NOTE: Sample dissolved some overnight, but next day, set aside and preserved EDTA supernatant, then split sample into three and replenished each tube with 10%. Stored overnight once more.
> - will combine ALL EDTA supernatant from all tubes into one 5 mL tube.
