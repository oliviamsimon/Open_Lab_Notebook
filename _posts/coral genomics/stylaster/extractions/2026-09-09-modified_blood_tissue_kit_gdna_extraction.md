## PROTOCOL

Decalcification

1. Fragment coral into small chunks.

2. Transfer fragment to a tube, taking care to avoid powder. Add 1,000 uL 15% EDTA (0.5M, pH 8) to fully submerge coral fragment.

3. Incubate for one hour in 4˚C, vortexing every 15 minutes to agitate.

4. Transfered to rotating incubator (Incubator Genie) @ 37˚C 10 rpm (speed 10) overnight.
NOTES 
- sample showed some bleached skeleton, skeleton shrunk a little bit. supernatant became more pink/orange
- removed fragments with cleaned forceps, then placed tube with powder and EDTA in rotating incubator to see if it dissolves more. Did not dissolve much more after 15 minutes. Refreshed EDTA in tube with fragments and placed back in incubator for overnight soak. 

4. Spin down sample 4,000 x g for 5 minutes. Remove supernatant. Spun down again for 1 min at 4000 x g. WEIGH TISSUE PELLET AND ADD TO TABLE!!!!

- NOTE: 5 minutes was not long enough of a spin, had to spin for an extra 6. Even after second spin, when I pipetted out supernatant, some debris came off wall. Second spin, spun at 10,000 x g for 5 mins to see if this fixes it or if it's an artifact of PBS and EDTA. 

5. Rinse twice in 1000 uL 1X PBS Buffer.

6. WEIGH REMAINING TISSUE PELLET AND ADD TO TABLE!!!!

7. Flash freeze in LN2 then proceed to homogenization.

Lysis

8. Add 720 µL of ATL buffer and 100 µL of Proteinase K. Mix by vortexing, then incubate for 2 hours at 56°C. 
- NOTES: sample completely clear, a little yellow tint

10. Add 800 µL of Buffer AL and mix by vortexing.

Binding 

3. Add 800 µL of ice-cold 100% ethanol and mix by vortexing. Transfer 650 uL of the mixture onto a spin column (you will need 3 spin columns per sample), centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through.


Washing

4. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW1, centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Repeat wash step.

5. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW2, centrifuge for 1 min at 17,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Repeat once more, but increase centrifuge time to 3 min.

6. Centrifuge again for 1 min at 17,000 × g to ensure membrane is completely dry.

Elution

6. Add 100 uL AE buffer directly onto the center of the filter. Let sit for 1 minutes. Elute DNA into a new tube by spinning down for 1 min at 6,000 x g. Repeat step by re-eluting eluate.
- NOTE: marked in tube as elute #1

7. Add 100 uL AE buffer directly onto the center of the filter again. Let sit for 5 minutes. Elute DNA into a new tube by spinning down for 1 min at 6,000 x g. Repeat step by re-eluting eluate.
- NOTE: marked in tube as elute #2

7. Add 20 µL of RNase A (10 mg/mL) to all tubes.

8. Incubate for 15 min at 37°C. In the meantime, chill isopropanol, prepare 75% ethanol and chill it as well. 

Cleanup

15. Add 10 uL (0.1 volume) 3M sodium acetate (pH 5.2) to sample, 1 uL glycoblue, and 275 µL (2.5 volume) of chilled isopropanol. Chill in 4˚C fridge for 30 minutes.

16. 10,000 x g, 4°C, 10 min until pellet is visible. Carefully remove supernatant, store in case transfer was unsuccessful. Do not disturb pellet.

17. Add 500 µL of chilled 75% ethanol (do not disturb the pellet) and centrifuge for 5 min at 10,000 × g at 4˚C. Remove supernatant.

18. Repeat Step 17. Dry sample in SpeedVac on low for 5 minutes.

19. Resuspend the pellet in 50 µL of nuclease-free water overnight at 4˚C.
