## PROTOCOL

Decalcification

1. Fragment coral into small chunks. Add small chunk of mostly tissue (5 polyps for SC2_1, way more polyps for SC2_2)

2. Add 720 µL of ATL buffer and 100 µL of Proteinase K. Mix by vortexing, then incubate overnight at 56°C. 
NOTE: after 24 hours, some white precipitate left, spun down sample at 4,000 rpm for 1 min @ 4˚C then transferred supernatant.

3. Split sample into two 400 uL aliquots

4. Add 400 uL AL and 400 uL chilled 100% ethanol

Binding 

5. Transfer 650 uL of the mixture onto a spin column (you will need 3 spin columns per sample), centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Do this until all sample is spun through

6. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW1, centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Repeat wash step.

7. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW2, centrifuge for 3 min at 17,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. 

8. Centrifuge again for 1 min at 17,000 × g to ensure membrane is completely dry.

Elution

9. Add 100 uL AE buffer directly onto the center of the filter. Let sit for 1 minutes. Elute DNA into a new tube by spinning down for 1 min at 6,000 x g. Repeat step by re-eluting eluate.
- NOTE: marked in tube as elute #1

10. Add 100 uL AE buffer directly onto the center of the filter again. Let sit for 5 minutes. Elute DNA into a new tube by spinning down for 1 min at 6,000 x g. Repeat step by re-eluting eluate.
- NOTE: marked in tube as elute #2
