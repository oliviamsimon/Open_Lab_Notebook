## OBJECTIVE
Salting out protocol for extraction of HMW, high quality, *Stylaster californicus* gDNA. Salting out extraction *should* remove protein and polysaccharide contamination more effectively, which has historically been an issue with this sample. Sample modified from https://www.authorea.com/doi/full/10.22541/au.170669467.78626984/v1 after personal communication with authors that have experience extracting from species from this family.

# REAGENTS

1. 1% Cell Lysis Buffer (350 uL x sample)
    a. 100mM Tris-Cl
    b. 50 mM EDTA
    c. 1% SDS
2. 70% Ethanol (400 uL x sample)

## PROTOCOL

Homogenization

1. Snap freeze in LN2 then grind in pre-cleaned mortar and pestle. Grind until baby powder like consistency.

Lysis

2. Add to each tube:
a.	350 µl 1% SDS cell lysis buffer
b.	42 µl 0.5 M EDTA 
c.	20 µl proteinase K

3. Mix carefully and incubate at 65˚C overnight.

4. Add 2 uL RNase A (100 mg/mL) and incubate on thermomixer at 37˚C for 30 minutes in the ThermoMixer 300 RPM

Protein/Cellular Debris Removal

5. Add 140 µl 7.5 M ammonium acetate to each tube. Mix by vortexing. Incubate at 4˚C for 10 minutes.

6. Centrifuge at 12,000 x g for 10 minutes.

7. Transfer supernatant to a new 1.5 ml microcentrifuge. Discard the previous tube.

8. Repeat steps 5-7.

Precipitation 

9. Add 700 µl cold isopropanol (volume ratio 1:1). Mix by inverting gently 50 times. Centrifuge at 8000 x g for 5 minutes.

10.	Carefully discard the supernatant, avoiding contact with the pellet. Drain the tube by placing on sterile blue roll, taking care that the pellet remains in the tube. 

Washing

11.	Add 400 µl 70% ethanol. Invert the tube several times to wash the DNA pellet. Centrifuge at 8000 x g for 1 minute. 

12.	Carefully discard the supernatant, avoiding contact with the pellet. If a lot of supernatant remains, pulse centrifuge the tubes and discard the supernatant using a smaller pipette, again avoiding contact with the pellet.

13.	Allow to air dry to 10-20 minutes. Non-contaminated pellets will turn more transparent as they dry.  Avoid over-drying the DNA pellet, as the DNA will be difficult to dissolve.

Rehydration

16.	Re-suspend dried pellets with 50 AE Buffer (Qiagen).  Invert tube to mix and spin down using centrifuge.

17.	Incubate at room temperature for 30 minutes or in the fridge overnight. 

18.	Briefly pulse centrifuge tubes and store at -20˚C.
