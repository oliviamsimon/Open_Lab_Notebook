## PROTOCOL

Decalcification

1. Fragment coral into small chunks.

2. Transfer fragment to a tube, taking care to avoid powder. Add 1,000 uL 15% EDTA (0.5M, pH 8) to fully submerge coral fragment.

3. Incubate for one hour in 4˚C, vortexing every 15 minutes to agitate.

4. Transfered to rotating incubator (Incubator Genie) @ 37˚C 10 rpm (speed 10) overnight.
NOTES 
- sample showed some bleached skeleton, skeleton shrunk a little bit. supernatant became more pink/orange
- removed fragments with cleaned forceps, then placed tube with powder and EDTA in rotating incubator to see if it dissolves more


4. Spin down sample 4,000 x g for 5 minutes. Remove supernatant. WEIGH TISSUE PELLET AND ADD TO TABLE!!!!

5. Rinse twice in 1000 uL 1X PBS Buffer.

6. WEIGH REMAINING TISSUE PELLET AND ADD TO TABLE!!!!

7. Flash freeze in LN2 then proceed to homogenization.

Homogenization

8. Briefly spin down sample in minicentrifuge (if tissue stuck on side of tube). For frozen samples, thaw at room temperature and quickly proceed to the next step.

8. Use the pestle to grind the sample within the pestle tube; leave the pestle in the tube. 

9. Using a wide-bore pipette tip, add 640 µl (Low Input: 320 µl) of the lysis master mix to the sample. Do not dispose of this tip yet, as it will be used to mix the sample.

10. Ensure there is no tissue material remaining on the pestle, then discard the pestle. If visible DNA threads or tissue material stick to the pestle, transfer it carefully into the tube by wiping the pestle tip along the tube rim.

11. Using the wide-bore tip, pipette up and down a few times to homogenize the tissue lysate and ensure rapid, complete lysis. Discard the pipette tip.


12. Incubate samples in a thermo mixer at 60°C for 30 minutes at 1000 rpm. (Low input: Incubate samples in a thermo mixer at 60 °C for 10 minutes at 1000 rpm, incubate another 20 minutes with no agitation) 

- Note: Invert the microtube a few times for every 5 minutes to ensure lysis. When lysis is complete, samples will turn from turbid to clear(or mostly clear, depending on the tissue type)

13. Add 20 µl (Low Input: 5 µl) RNase A and mix by inverting 5–10 times. Incubate for 10 minutes at 56°C at 1000 rpm (the same agitation speed used in Step 5). 

14. Chill sample on ice for 3 minutes.

15. Add 650 μL(low input:325 μL) Chloroform: Isoamyl Alcohol(24:1, v/v)

16. Mix the tube vigorously by inversion or gentle vortexing for 30-60 second

17. Centrifuge at 16,000 x g for 10 minutes at room temperature

18. Carefully transfer the upper aqueous layer(~600 μL) into a new microfuge tube.

19. Add 300 µl (Low Input: 150 µl) Protein Separation Solution and mix by inverting for 1 minute.

20. Centrifuge for 10 minutes at 16,000 x g. 

- Note: The sample will separate into a large, clear phase (DNA) and a smaller (often brown) protein phase, usually on the bottom of the tube. For some tissues, the protein phase may be yellow or clear. Additional centrifugation time (10–20 minutes) may be required for complete phase separation, particularly when low agitation speeds were used.

21. If working with multiple samples, prepare and label the plastics for the upcoming steps. Each sample will require:

- 1 Monarch Collection Tube II (no need to label)
- 1 Monarch Bead Retainer inserted into the collection tube; this will be used to remove the wash buffer from the gDNA bound to the beads.
- 2 Monarch 2 ml Tubes; one for phase separation and one for elution.
- 1 1.5 ml microfuge tube (DNA low bind recommended, not provided); this will be used to collect the eluate.

22. Using a 1000 µl (Low Input: 200 µl) wide-bore pipette tip, transfer the upper phase containing the DNA (large, clear phase) to a labeled Monarch 2 ml Tube.

-NOTE: Using a 200 µl wide-bore pipette tip to transfer the final volume of the upper phase is recommended for maximum yield. Avoid transferring material from the protein layer, although a small amount (1–2 µl) will not be detrimental. If a small amount of the protein phase enters the pipette tip, gently push it back into the tube. If a lower protein phase is not visible, leave ~30 µl behind to ensure protein is not carried over. Typically, the transferred volume will be ~ 800 µl (Low Input: ~400 µl). If the volume of the sample is < 700 µl (Low Input: < 350 µl), adjust the volume of isopropanol used in Step 2 of Part 2: HMW gDNA Binding and Elution to 0.7 volumes.

23. Add 560 μL (0.7 volume Isopropanol) and gently invert the tube 2-3 times to mix.

24. Centrifuge at 12000 x g for 1 minute to pellet free-floating DNA.

25. Carefully remove supernatant.

26. Add 500 μL Wash Buffer, carefully invert the tube 2-3 times

27. Spin down pellet at 12000 x g for 1 minute and remove as much supernatant as possible.

28. Pulse-spin in minicentrifuge and remove residual wash buffer.

29. Invert the tube on a small Kimwipe and air dry at room temperature for 5 minutes.

30. Add 100 μL Elution Buffer II and incubate for a minimum of 5 minutes at 56 °C in a thermo mixer with agitation of 300 rpm.

31. Pipette eluate up and down 5–10 times with a wide bore pipette tip and ensure any visible DNA aggregates are dispersed. Before analysis or downstream use, HMW DNA must be homogeneously dissolved. 

32. Incubate DNA samples for 30-60 minutes at 37 °C. 

33. Pipet up and down 5-10 times again using the same wide bore tip.

Binding 

22. Add 410 µL of ice-cold 100% ethanol and mix by vortexing. Transfer 650 uL of the mixture onto a spin column (you will need 3 spin columns per sample), centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through.


Washing

4. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW1, centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Repeat wash step.

5. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW2, centrifuge for 1 min at 17,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Repeat once more but spin for 3 minutes.

6. Centrifuge again for 1 min at 17,000 × g to ensure membrane is completely dry.

Elution

6. Add 90 uL AE buffer directly onto the center of the filter. Let sit for 5 minutes. Elute DNA into a new tube by spinning down for 1 min at 6,000 x g. Repeat step by re-eluting eluate.

7. Add 4 µL of RNase A.

8. Incubate for 15 min at 37°C. In the meantime, chill isopropanol, prepare 75% ethanol and chill it as well. 

Cleanup

15. Add 10 uL (0.1 volume) 3M sodium acetate (pH 5.2) to sample, 1 uL glycoblue, and 210== µL of chilled isopropanol. Chill in 4˚C fridge for 30 minutes.

16. 10,000 x g, 4°C, 10 min until pellet is visible. Carefully remove supernatant, store in case transfer was unsuccessful. Do not disturb pellet.

17. Add 500 µL of chilled 75% ethanol (do not disturb the pellet) and centrifuge for 5 min at 10,000 × g at 4˚C. Remove supernatant.

18. Repeat Step 17.

19. Resuspend the pellet in 50 µL of nuclease-free water overnight at 4˚C.
