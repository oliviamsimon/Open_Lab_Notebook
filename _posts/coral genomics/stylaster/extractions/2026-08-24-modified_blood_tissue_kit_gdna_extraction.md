## SAMPLES

08/19/2026 sc1

* Fragment was added to tube with 10% EDTA, soaked overnight at 4˚C, spun down at ~4,250 x g's for 10 mins @ 4˚C then flash frozen and stored in -80˚C freezer. 

## PROTOCOL

1. Thaw sample, then rinse in ddH2O by adding 1000 uL ddH2O, vortexing, centrifuging at 4,000 × g for 5 min at 4˚C, then discarding supernatant. Repeat once more for a total of two washes.

Notes
- sample mass after final ddh2o removal: 4.5564 g
- pinkish/orange pellet at bottom of tube after spinning down

Lysis

1. Add 720 µL of ATL buffer and 100 µL of Proteinase K. Mix by vortexing, then incubate for 30 min at 56°C. Lower incubator temperature to 37˚C for RNase step when you remove samples.

NOTES
- after 20 mins, white strings visible. sample mostly clear after 30 mins.

2. Add 800 µL of Buffer AL and mix by vortexing.

- viscous solution

Binding 

3. Add 800 µL of ice-cold 100% ethanol and mix by vortexing. Transfer 650 uL of the mixture onto a spin column (you will need two spin columns per sample), centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through.

- viscous again
- split into two spin columns for speed
- empty tube mass: 4.5786, starting material about 200 mg

Washing

4. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW1, centrifuge for 1 min at 6,000 × g, and discard the flow-through.

5. Add 500 µL of Buffer AW2, centrifuge for 3 min at 17,000 × g, and discard the flow-through. Centrifuge again for 1 min at 17,000 × g to ensure membrane is completely dry


Elution

6. Add 90 uL AE buffer directly onto the center of the filter. Let sit for 5 minutes. Elute DNA into a new tube by spinning down for 1 min at 6,000 x g. Repeat step by re-eluting eluate.

7. Add 2 µL of RNase A.

8. Incubate for 15 min at 37°C. In the meantime, chill isopropanol, prepare 75% ethanol and chill it as well. 

Cleanup

15. Add 10 uL (0.1 volume) 3M sodium acetate (pH 5.2) to sample, 1 uL glycoblue, and 150 µL of chilled isopropanol. Chill in 4˚C fridge for 30 minutes.

16. 10,000 x g, 4°C, 10 min until pellet is visible. Carefully remove supernatant, store in case transfer was unsuccessful. Do not disturb pellet.

17. Add 500 µL of chilled 75% ethanol (do not disturb the pellet) and centrifuge for 5 min at 10,000 × g at 4˚C. Remove supernatant.

18. Repeat Step 17.

19. Resuspend the pellet in 50 µL of nuclease-free water.
