## OBJECTIVE

Clean contaminants from sample following DNA extraction. 

Preparation
1. Aliquot isopropanol and place in -20˚C to chill.
2. Prepare 70% ethanol and place in freezer.
> Same volume as isopropanol, but washed 3x, so 3 volumes per sample 

Precipitation
3. Add 5 uL volume 3M sodium acetate (pH 5.2) to sample and 1 uL glycoblue
4. Mix by gentle inversion (don't vortex)
5. Add 33 uL ice cold isopropanol
6. Mix by gentle inversion until DNA precipitate is visible (stringy/white)
7. Centrifuge at 10,000 x g, 4°C, 10 min until pellet is visible. Carefully remove supernatant, store in case transfer was unsuccessful. Do not disturb pellet.

Wash
8. Add 33 uL fresh ice-cold 70% ethanol to rinse pellet
9. Centrifuge at 10,000 x g for 5-10 min at 4°C. 
10. Repeat 1x more for a total of 2 washes
11. Air dry for 5-10 minutes until translucent (not fully clear/glassy — avoid over-drying).
12. Resuspend in molecular-grade water in 4˚C.

Storage
13. Store in -20˚C until future use.
