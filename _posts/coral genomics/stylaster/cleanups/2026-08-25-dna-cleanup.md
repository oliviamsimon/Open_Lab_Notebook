## OBJECTIVE

Cleanup polysaccharide/protein contamination in extracted DNA samples.

## SAMPLES

8/24/2026 SC1_A_1
8/24e/2026 SC1_A_2

Preparation
1. Place 100% EtOH in freezer to chill
2. Prepare 70% ethanol and place in freezer.
3. Bring sample volume up to 200 uL by adding 161 uL ddH2O to sample. 
4. Add 200 uL phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (PCI) to sample and invert to mix well
5. Centrifuge at room temperature for 5 minutes at 16,000 × g. Carefully remove the upper aqueous phase and transfer the layer to a fresh tube. Be sure not to carry over any phenol during pipetting.

Precipitation
6. Add (equal volume) 12 uL 3M sodium acetate (pH 5.2) to sample and 1 uL glycoblue
4. Mix by gentle inversion (don't vortex)
5. Add 120 uL ice cold 100% ethanol
6. Mix by gentle inversion. Place in -20˚C freezer overnight.
7. Centrifuge at 10,000 x g, 4°C, 10 min until pellet is visible, carefully remove supernatant.
8. Add 150 μL of 70% ethanol. Centrifuge at 10,000 x g for 5-10 min at 4°C. 
9. Repeat 1x more for a total of 2 washes.
10. Air dry for 5-10 minutes until translucent (not fully clear/glassy — avoid over-drying).
11. Resuspend in molecular-grade water in 4˚C.

Storage
13. Store in -20˚C until future use.
